DO NOT USE THIS SIMULATOR TO PLAN SURGERY FOR A REAL PATIENT!!!

This software is currently under development and has not been calibrated with data from fresh cadavers or live patients at this time.  To be accurate for specific patients, data should be collected from the patient with 3D scanning and biophysical measurements.  At present this software is intended for
educational purposes only.  This program makes no guarantees regarding surgical validity.  Surgical planning for any specific patient is strictly the responsibility of the surgeon.
